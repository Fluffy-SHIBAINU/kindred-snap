When real bills are switched on:

You can cover your name, address and ID numbers first, with a marker or your phone's markup tool. Anything you leave showing travels with the picture to Kindred and the reading service. We use only the amounts, dates, service codes and the hospital's name.

Kindred and the reading service receive each page as it looks when you drop it. What you covered solidly does not reach us. What you left showing does.

What happens to my files

A marker or highlighter can show through, even when it looks solid to you. A solid black box, or paper laid over the words before you take the photo, does not.

Kindred keeps no copy of your files and stores no personal details. We count that a private check happened and how it ended, by month, and nothing else.

This is not anonymous. A hospital, a date and an amount can point to one visit, and our web host records that this phone connected: internet address, time and page.
