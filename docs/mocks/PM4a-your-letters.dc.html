4. What We AssumedKeep this page. Do not send it.

Where your files didn't show something, we made a best guess and marked it Assumed. Nothing assumed is written in a letter as a fact.

Assumed

One person lives in the home.

If two to four people live there, the policy lists free care. The letter leaves household size blank and asks the hospital which tier applies.

Assumed

We took this to be Original Medicare, because you dropped a Medicare notice.

The letters name the notice, never the coverage.

Attach a copy of the bill and the notice. Send it all to Patient Financial Services, at the address printed on the bill.
