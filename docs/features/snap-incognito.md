# Snap Incognito (Private Mode) — Feature Plan

Plan of 2026-11-16, written to the founder's direction of that day: "There are no typing or changes. All the user does is just dropping files like Snap, but incognito can handle redacted data (user's name etc, but we are assuming the income is shown)." It replaces the plan of 2026-11-15 and that plan's red-team recommendations where they conflict (§16). Nothing here is built. The runbook form is `docs/BUILD.md` Phase 10 (I1 to I4, twenty tasks). Items marked **unverified** were not checked by anyone and are tasks, not facts.

## 1. What Users Asked For and the Promise
Feedback: some users will not share a whole bill. They want to drop a copy they already redacted, or have us redact it, in one flow, and still get a best-shot result that carries no sensitive details. The founder asked for one more button on Snap, "Incognito — Private Mode", and no typing. The plan: the button opens the Snap screen in private mode at `/private`. The user takes a photo or picks files: the bill and whatever came with it (the Medicare notice or insurer's statement, an income letter). Names, addresses and ID numbers may be covered, partly covered or not covered at all; all three go through the same flow. We use amounts, dates, service codes and the hospital's name; every gap gets a best guess that is shown and labelled; the letters leave a blank wherever a personal detail or a guess would go.

**The promise.** Each sentence below holds for covered, partly covered and uncovered files alike, because of how the code is built, and a test proves it. They are the complete set of promise sentences shown to users. They live in one file, `src/lib/private/copy.ts`, and `tests/private/copy.test.ts` asserts each appears verbatim in this document (S7 and S14 as templates, with their slots).

| Id | Where | Sentence | True because |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | `/snap`, in the disclosure under the button | Private mode checks a bill with your name and numbers covered, or as it is. Kindred and the reading service receive whatever is showing. We use only the amounts, dates, service codes and the hospital's name, and Kindred keeps no copy. It is not anonymous. | §5: memory only, no store write, amounts-only schema |
| S2 | `/private`, body, always visible | You can cover your name, address and ID numbers first, with a marker or your phone's markup tool. Anything you leave showing travels with the picture to Kindred and the reading service. We use only the amounts, dates, service codes and the hospital's name. | The reader's schema has no other key; `boundRead` rebuilds from an allow-list; `scrub` runs over what was read |
| S3 | `/private`, body, always visible | Kindred and the reading service receive each page as it looks when you drop it. What you covered solidly does not reach us. What you left showing does. | From I1 the phone redraws every page as a new picture before upload (`hygiene.ts`); the original file, its hidden details and any leftover data from before an edit are never sent; a file that does not decode as a picture is refused on the phone |
| S4 | `/private`, "What happens to my files" | A marker or highlighter can show through, even when it looks solid to you. A solid black box, or paper laid over the words before you take the photo, does not. | Stated limit; no code hides see-through ink before I4 |

| S8 | `/snap` disclosure and first on `/private`, demo reader only | Demo: only the demo files are read. Any other file stays on this phone. | S10 | Result, "What We Assumed" | Where your files didn't show something, we made a best guess and marked it Assumed. Nothing assumed is written in a letter as a fact. | §6 rule and its tests |

| S15 | Hard stop | Kindred kept nothing from these files. | §5: the bytes live for one request |
| S16 | Failure on our side | Something went wrong on our side, not with your files. Kindred kept nothing from them. Try again in a few minutes. | Every error returns one fixed status; only "the reader could not read it" is ever called blurry |

`src/lib/private/readerTerms.ts` ships as `READER_TERMS = { provider: "", service: "", retentionDays: null, trains: null, staffMayReview: null, url: "", readOn: "", goLiveApprovedOn: "" }`. A person fills it in from the provider's current terms; no figure is pre-filled, so none can be approved by default. `PRIVATE_READER=gemini` throws at startup while any field is empty or `trains` is not `false`; when `staffMayReview` is true S7 gains "Its staff may look at them to check for abuse."

**What the mode does not claim.** It is not anonymous and not "de-identified". It makes no HIPAA statement. It does not say an uncovered identifier is hidden from Kindred's server or from the reading service: both receive the picture, and S2 and S3 say so before any file is chosen. It does not say automatic covering (I4) catches everything. It decides nothing about eligibility: the hospital does. It sends nothing to a hospital; the user completes and sends the letters, and nothing is ever marked sent.

## 2. The Flow
Four screens, the same ones Snap uses: Snap, We're On It, Result, Letters. The income screen is skipped, because income is read from the dropped documents. `/private` holds the steps in client state with nothing in the URL. Titles are Title Case; buttons and body are sentence case (the mode's name on its button is the one exception, kept as the founder wrote it); one primary action per screen.
- **The user's only actions:** (1) tap `Incognito — Private Mode` on Snap; (2) take a photo or pick files, as often as they have pages; (3) wait; (4) tap `Get the letters`; (5) print, save or share; (6) optionally tap `Erase from this phone`. There is no text, number or date input, no select, no radio or tick control, no question, no list to choose a hospital from, no crop or cover tool and no explainer screen. Every `<input>` the route renders is a file input (no `<form>` is used, so there are no hidden inputs either), and a test asserts it on every step. When something needed cannot be read, the only remedy offered is another file.
- **Screen 1a, `/snap` (existing).** One addition: a full-width secondary link `Incognito — Private Mode` (`<a href="/private">`, `SECONDARY_CLASS`) under "Pick a PDF or photo instead", inside the first viewport at 390 by 844 (the camera guide shrinks if needed). Under it a `<details>` with the summary `What is private mode?` holding S1 (demo reader: S8, then S1). Nothing else on Snap changes.
- **Screen 1b, `/private`: Snap in private mode.** h1 "Drop the Bill and What Came With It". Body: "The hospital bill and, if you have them, the Medicare notice or your insurer's statement, and an income letter. One page per photo." Then, visible without opening anything: S2, S3 and, with the live reader, S7. With the demo reader S8 comes first at body size and S2 and S3 follow under the muted caption "When real bills are switched on:". Until I2: "PDFs come next. For now, drop a photo or a screenshot." A `<details>` with the summary `What happens to my files` holds S4, S5, S6 and, with the live reader, a link to the privacy notice. The controls mirror Snap: primary in the action bar `Take photo` (opens the camera, one page per shot); secondary `Pick photos or files instead` (`multiple`, `accept="image/*,application/pdf"`); text action, demo reader only, `Use the demo files`. The camera guide stays and is also a drop target on desktop. **The check starts when a file arrives. There is no file list, no remove control and no start button.** **Adding pages.** `PrivateFlow` keeps the prepared pages in component memory (a ref, never storage). Every later photo or pick is added to them and the same single request runs again with all of them, so a bill photographed now and a notice photographed next end in one result. Limits, checked on the phone after redrawing: 10 pages and 4 MB together ("Up to 10 pages in one check. Drop fewer."). After a reload the result is restored but the pages are gone, so `Add another file` becomes `Start over`.
- **The hard stop.** One value cannot be guessed: the amount owed. Without it Screen 1b shows again with a `role="status"` box and S15; the pages already dropped are kept, so the next file completes them. Wording by cause: "The amount you owe is covered. Drop a copy that shows it."; "We couldn't find a hospital bill in these files. Drop the bill."; "We couldn't read the amount you owe. Drop a clearer copy."; "These look like two different bills. Drop one bill at a time, with its notice." (this one clears the held pages); every file unreadable: Snap's "The numbers are too blurry to trust, so we won't use them."; an error on our side: S16. Refused on the phone, with no request: a PDF before I2 (the sentence above); a file that is not a picture ("This phone saved the photo in a format we can't open here. Take a screenshot of it and drop that."); with the demo reader, any non-demo file (S8).
- **Screen 2, "We're On It".** "Nothing for you to do yet. This page updates on its own." Five `StepRow`s with private titles from `copy.ts`: "1. Read your files", "2. Notice or statement", "3. Compare lines", "4. Help program", "5. Write letters". The first is active with "Reading your files" while the one request runs; when it returns each row shows a summary built by `privateSteps(result)` from a fixed table ("No notice in your files", "For a household of one, the policy lists 75% off. Assumed.", "Letters ready to print.") and the screen moves on by itself. No button. From I4, S9 when covering ran.

**Screen 3, the result.** One h1, the first row that applies. "The notice" reads "the Medicare notice", "your insurer's statement" or "the notice or statement" according to what was dropped; the word Medicare appears only when a Medicare notice was among the files.

## 3. What Is Used, What Is Ignored
| Class | What | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Never taken off the page | Name, address, date of birth, phone, email; account, claim, guarantor, record and Medicare numbers; barcodes and QR codes; service descriptions and notice notes | The reader's schema has no key for any of them. On an uncovered page the reading service sees them in the picture; they are never asked for, returned, stored or shown |
| Never leaves the phone | The original file, its name, its hidden details (time, place, device, preview, leftover data); with the demo reader, any file at all | `hygiene.ts` uploads a redrawn JPEG named `file`; the demo reader gets ids only |
| Used as a lookup key only | The hospital's name, city and state (from the bill, else from the notice) | Matched against the atlas (§5); the browser gets the atlas's own spelling or nothing. The read strings never leave the server |
| Read and returned to the phone | Amount owed; per line: number, service code, service date, charge; on a notice: amount billed and "you may be billed" per line, the maximum (or "your responsibility" on an insurer's statement), how many claims it lists; statement date, due date; income amount and its period as printed; what kind of document each file is | Bounded and rebuilt key by key (§5) |
| Worked out | The gap, which lines pair, the hospital match, yearly income from a monthly figure, percent of the poverty guideline, the tier, the estimate | Chip "We worked this out"; money keeps the one label Estimated |
| Assumed | Household size (one person), coverage type (from the kind of statement), that the notice is for the same visit, the guideline region (48 states and DC) | Chip Assumed; listed in "What We Assumed"; never in a letter (§6) |
| Never stored | All of the above; the file bytes; the result; a case id, case code, owner, consent record, audit event or staff queue row (none is created) | The bytes live in memory for one request. The result lives in the browser tab. The one stored value is a count of private checks by month and how each ended, in `meta` (§5) |
| Logged by the host | Internet address, user agent, time, path `/private` | Unavoidable; S6. Whether the host records request bodies is **unverified** (§9) |

## 5. Server Side
One server action, one request, nothing kept. `src/app/(caregiver)/private/actions.ts`:
```ts
export async function runPrivate(form: FormData): Promise<PrivateOutcome>
// form: "file" × 1 to 10 (JPEG pictures redrawn on the phone; live and stub readers only), or "demo" × 1 to 10 (demo file ids; fixture reader only)
type PrivateOutcome = { status: "ok"; result: PrivateResult } | { status: "needs_amount"; reason: "covered" | "no_bill" | "not_found" | "two_bills" }
  | { status: "unreadable"; reason: "demo_only" | "too_dark" } | { status: "failed" };
```
It reads each part into memory (JPEG magic bytes, 10 parts and 4 MB at most, else `failed`), calls the reader once per file in parallel, bounds and scrubs each answer, runs the stateless investigation, increments the count and returns. `too_dark` is returned only when the reader itself reports the pages unreadable. One outer try/catch turns every other error (the reading service down, a quota, a timeout, a bug, a letter that fails its number check) into `{ status: "failed" }` with no message text, because Next logs uncaught action errors and `toCents` puts the offending input in its message. No `console.*`, analytics or tracing call exists on this path; `next.config.ts` sets `logging: { serverFunctions: false }`. The part's file name and type string are never read.

**The reading result** (`src/lib/private/types.ts`; `src/lib/types.ts` is unchanged). The reader classifies each file itself; nobody tells it what was dropped.
```ts
type DocKind = "bill" | "msn" | "eob" | "income" | "other" | "unreadable";
type Needed = "patient_owes" | "hospital" | "service_date" | "line_code" | "line_charge" | "line_patient" | "statement_date" | "due_date" | "notice_max" | "income";
type PrivateRead = {
  kind: DocKind; page: number;                                                  // page = position in drop order, set by us
  hospital: { name: string; city: string | null; state: string | null } | null; // lookup key only; never returned
  lines: Array<{ n: number; code: string | null; serviceDate: string | null; chargeCents: number | null; billedCents: number | null; youMayBeBilledCents: number | null }>;
  patientOwesCents: number | null;
  noticeMaxCents: number | null; claimCount: number | null;                     // "Maximum You May Be Billed", or "your responsibility" on an eob
  statementDate: string | null; dueDate: string | null;
  income: { cents: number; period: "week" | "two_weeks" | "month" | "year" | "other" } | null;   // the total as printed; the yearly total when the page prints one
  covered: Needed[];                                                            // needed values the reader saw under a cover
};
```
- **The reader** (`src/lib/adapters/extract/privateRead.ts`): `PrivateReader { read(input: { bytes: Uint8Array } | { demoId: string }): Promise<unknown> }`. `FixturePrivateReader` answers demo ids from `fixtures/private/reads.json`; an unknown id is `demo_only`. `StubPrivateReader` (e2e only; refused in production without `ALLOW_DEMO=1`) answers any JPEG with the uncovered demo reads by position, so the upload path can be tested without a key. `GeminiPrivateReader` (I3) sends a fixed prompt and the picture as inline data only (rule 5), temperature 0, with a `responseJsonSchema` that mirrors `PrivateRead`: amounts as printed strings, dates, codes, integers and enums. The hospital's name and city are its only free-text keys. Whether a model reports `covered`, `claimCount` and the document kind reliably on real documents is **unverified** and is measured before go-live.
- **Bounding and scrubbing** (`src/lib/private/scrub.ts`). `boundRead(raw: unknown, page: number, today: string): PrivateRead` rebuilds the object key by key from an allow-list: `n` an integer 1 to 99; `code` matching `^(\d{4,5}|[A-Z]\d{4}|\d{4}[A-Z])(-[A-Z0-9]{2})?$`; an amount only when printed with two decimals (`^\$?\d{1,3}(,\d{3})*\.\d{2}$`) and under $1,000,000, which rejects an account number; service and statement dates within the three years before today, a due date no more than one year after it (which rejects a date of birth); `claimCount` an integer 1 to 20; `covered` filtered to the `Needed` list; a hospital name or city of at most 80 letters, spaces and `&.'-`, with no digit. Anything else becomes null. `scrub<T>(value: T): T` then replaces anything shaped like a Medicare number, a Social Security number, an email, a phone number or a run of six or more digits with "[removed]" in the bounded reads and the composed evidence lines, before any atlas data is joined; policy text, phones, links and CCNs from the atlas are never scrubbed. On the fixtures it must replace nothing.
- **Hospital match** (`atlas.ts`, new `matchHospital(h, sheets?)`): only when both name and state were read. Candidates are the sheets in that state whose normalised name equals the read name; else, when the city was read, sheets in that state and city where one name contains the other. Exactly one candidate is a match (`exact` true only for the first rule); anything else is T4. The demo partner is matched by name only when `demo` is true; its synthetic coverage sentence lives in `fixtures/private/demo-policy.json`.

**The stateless run** (`src/lib/private/join.ts`, `run.ts`):
```ts
export function join(reads: PrivateRead[]): Joined | { stop: "covered" | "no_bill" | "not_found" | "two_bills" | "too_dark" }   // T12, T13, T16 to T20
export function assume(j: Joined, policy: PrivatePolicy | null): Assumption[]                                // every default of §6, in one place
export function outcomeOf(findings: Finding[], j: Joined): PrivateResult["outcome"]
export async function runStateless(reads: PrivateRead[], deps: { match(h: PrivateRead["hospital"]): PrivatePolicy | null; today: string; demo: boolean }, householdSize: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 = 1): Promise<PrivateOutcome>
```
1. `join` the reads. A stop returns `needs_amount` or `unreadable`.
2. `const store = new MemoryStore()`: a local variable, never `getStore()` or `getServerStore()`, gone when the function returns. It receives one throwaway `Case` (id `"private"`, no code, `patientName: "[Patient name]"`, no consent, income only when read per month or year, with the assumed household size) and one amounts-only bill `Document` (empty `fields`, empty descriptions, blank strings for covered codes and dates, `SourceRef` quotes composed from allow-listed values such as "Line 3, Q9967, $240.00").
3. `runInvestigation(store, "private", { claims, policyFor, now, drafts }, { tolerant: true })`, the existing pipeline. `claims` is an in-line connector of kind `"dropped_document"` whose `fetchNotice` returns the dropped notice as a `Document`, or null. `policyFor` returns null when the policy excludes insured patients and a statement was dropped, so no percent is ever computed for it. `drafts` is `privateDrafts` (§7).
4. A case that did not reach `ready_to_send` returns `failed`, never a partial packet. Otherwise read back `eligibility`, `store.findingsFor` and `store.actionsFor`; add the household table with `fplPercent` and `tierFor` for one to four people and the estimate with `estimateOpportunity` (parts relabelled "Line the notice shows as not yours to pay" and "Help listed for a household of one"); build the five rows with `privateSteps` (the pipeline's own summaries and every `Finding.explanation` are discarded: they speak of asking Medicare, a billing specialist and approval, none of which exists here); assemble `PrivateResult`.

**Small edits to shared code**, each off by default so regular Snap is unchanged and `tests/pipeline.test.ts` passes untouched:

## 6. Assumptions
The founder's direction is a best shot on stated assumptions with no questions. Each gap in what the files show gets one rule. Every rule that applied to a run is an `Assumption`, listed under "What We Assumed" with the chip Assumed.
```ts
type AssumptionKey = "household_size" | "coverage_type" | "insurance_unknown" | "same_visit" | "hospital" | "hospital_match" | "service_date" | "line_code" | "line_charge" | "line_patient" | "statement_date" | "due_date" | "no_notice" | "notice_lines_sum" | "no_income" | "income_period" | "income_added" | "second_statement" | "guideline_region";
type Assumption = { key: AssumptionKey; statement: string; ifWrong: string; inLetter: "blank" | "asks" | "left_out" };
```
| What is missing | Best shot | How the result shows it | What the letter prints instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Household size (always: we never read it) | One person | The one-to-four table; the estimate "if the household is one person" | "Household size: ____"; no percent and no tier; it asks which tier applies |
| Coverage type | Read off the kind of statement: a Medicare Summary Notice means Original Medicare; an insurer's statement means a plan; none dropped means not known | "We took this to be Original Medicare, because you dropped a Medicare notice." or "We couldn't tell whether the patient has insurance." | Names the document, never the coverage; tick boxes "[ ] The Medicare notice or insurer's statement for this visit is attached   [ ] I have not received one" |
| Same visit | Yes, when at least one line pairs on code and date; otherwise assumed, confidence "possible" | "We took the notice to be for the same visit as the bill." | "Please confirm this notice covers the same visit." |
| Hospital | None; no policy lookup. A match by containment is shown, not asserted | "We couldn't tell which hospital." or "If this is not your hospital, ignore the help program and the phone number here." | "[Hospital name]" unless the name matched exactly; no assistance letter without a match |
| Service date; a line's code; a line's charge | Pair on what is left (T5, T6); a covered charge is never paired (T16) | "possible" on the finding | Blank date; the line is named by its number and charge |
| Statement date; due date | None | No due-date alert | "[statement date]", "[due date]" |
| The notice | None; comparison "insufficient information" | The first h1 of §2 | Information request; the tick boxes above |
| The notice's maximum (lines visible), or several claims | The sum of the paired lines | "We added up the lines we could match." | The lines by number; no total is printed |
| Income | None; a weekly or fortnightly figure is treated as not read | The policy's ceilings table | "Total yearly household income: ____" |
| Two income documents that differ | Added together for the table | Says so | Each figure as printed; no sum |
| Guideline region | The 2026 guideline for the 48 states and DC (the only table in `fpl.ts`) | Stated; a hospital in Alaska or Hawaii gets no percent | Nothing |
| The amount owed | No guess: the hard stop | | |

- **The household table.** Whenever income was read, the result shows one row per household size from one to four: the percent of the guideline and the tier the policy lists. With the demo files ($37,560.00 a year, demo policy): one person, 235.3%, 75% off; two, 173.6%, free care; three, 137.5%, free care; four, 113.8%, free care. A wrong assumption is visible at a glance, and one person is the cautious end wherever a policy's discounts fall as income rises (true of the demo policy; **unverified** across the atlas, so the table always shows all four rows and the heading carries a percent only when all four agree).
- **The rule, and its tests.** No assumed value, and nothing derived from one, appears in a letter as a statement of fact. `tests/private/letters.test.ts` proves it: (a) `runStateless` with household size 1 and 4 gives identical letters, character for character, also for an income above the one-person top tier; (b) every dollar figure in a letter equals a value in a `PrivateRead` (no sum, product or difference); (c) outside the quoted policy sentences no letter contains a percent sign; (d) no letter contains "waiting", and none contains "Medicare" unless a Medicare notice was among the reads; (e) when the deciding pair is not code-and-date the letter contains "Please confirm this notice covers the same visit." and never "same code and date".
- **Labels.** One chip per value: **Read from your file**, **We worked this out**, **Assumed**, **Quoted** (the hospital's published policy words, with link and checked-on date), **Demo files**. Money has exactly one label, **Estimated**; confirmed and actual cannot exist in this mode. A real policy block is headed "Real hospital policy, checked <date>"; the demo policy carries the demo badge (rule 6).

## 7. Outputs
- **Result blocks, in order:** the h1 and its line (§2) · the evidence pair in `Clipping`s set in the body face and captioned "Our reading of line 3, not a copy of the page" (composed from allow-listed values, so weaker than regular Snap's verbatim quote, and the caption says so) · the money block with the Estimated badge and "Estimated. The hospital decides. If the household is one person." · the h2 and the policy block: the match line, the policy's quote, the coverage sentence ("The policy we read does not say whether it covers patients who have insurance. The hospital decides." when not stated), and "Other Conditions in the Policy" with the quoted asset or residency rule · the household table · "What We Assumed" (S10, then each assumption and what happens if it is wrong, then any left-out files) · the due-date alert when a due date was read · help (§2) · S11 and `Erase from this phone`. `PrivateResultView` writes its own sentences from `outcome`, `help`, `noticeKind` and amounts; it never prints pipeline text.
- **Coverage decides whether a percent may appear at all.** `not_covered`: no percent, no tier, no estimate part, anywhere in the serialised result. `insurance_unknown`: the ceilings table, no single percent. `not_stated`: the percent shows with the sentence above. An asset or residency rule: the percent shows only with the rule quoted directly under it. `no_policy`, Alaska and Hawaii: no percent.
- **The packet** is built on the server in the same request and returned inside `PrivateResult.letters`; opening it makes no request. (1) Cover sheet "Before You Send": what is still blank; what to attach (a copy of the bill, a copy of the notice, proof of income if applying); where it goes (Patient Financial Services at the address printed on the bill; the atlas phone and form link, each with its source and the match line); and "If you send this for someone else, the hospital may ask for the patient's written permission. Have the patient sign too, or attach their permission." (2) The correction letter (outcome `line_found` or `line_possible`) or the information request (`amounts_differ`, `no_notice`, `notice_covered`, `notice_unmatched`). (3) The assistance application, whenever a policy was matched and coverage is not `not_covered`, whatever the tier. (4) "What We Assumed", marked "Keep this page. Do not send it."

**Letter wording** (`src/lib/private/letters.ts`, `privateDrafts`; the regular templates keep their wording). Every letter passes the existing `validateDraft` against the facts it returns.
- Reference: "Re: account number [account number], statement dated [statement date], patient [Patient name]" (a date that was read is printed).
- Signature block: "[ ] I am the patient   [ ] I am helping the patient", then ruled lines "Name", "Signature" and "Patient's signature, or attach the patient's written permission". - Correction, paired on code and date: "Bill line 3: Q9967 $240.00" and "The notice, same code and date: nothing the patient may be billed." (in words, because `validateDraft` rejects a "$0.00" that is not a case figure). Paired any other way: "Bill line 3 ($240.00) appears to match an item the notice shows with nothing the patient may be billed. Please review it." and the same-visit sentence.
- Information request: "We have not been able to compare each line of the statement with the notice. Please send an itemized bill for this account and the claim adjustment reason codes returned for each line."
- Assistance: every published sentence of the policy quoted in policy order (free care, tiers, application window), whatever the tier; "The enclosed income letter shows $37,560.00 a year." (figure and period as printed, one sentence per income document), then "Total yearly household income: ____" and "Household size: ____"; the notice tick boxes; and "Please tell us which tier of your published financial assistance policy, if any, applies to this household."
- **Placeholders**, printed as a ruled blank with the label under it: `[Patient name]`, `[account number]`, `[Hospital name]`, `[statement date]`, `[due date]`. None is figure-shaped, so `validateDraft` passes them; a unit test pins the list.
- **Getting it off the phone.** `Print or save as PDF` calls `window.print()`; the print stylesheet sets the shell height to auto and the main region's overflow to visible (the caregiver shell is `h-dvh` with an inner scroller), hides the header and action bar, and breaks one letter per page. `Share` uses `navigator.share` with the letter text where the browser has it. `Copy the text` uses the clipboard. - **The tab.** `src/lib/private/session.ts` keeps the `PrivateResult` in sessionStorage under `snap_private_v1` so a reload does not lose it, purges it after 30 idle minutes (the held pages with it), and `forget()` clears both. Nothing else is stored in the browser.

## 10. Threat Model and Residual Risks
**Assets:** identity; account access (account and Medicare numbers); health detail (codes and dates); financial detail (amounts, income). **Adversaries:** Kindred staff or a database breach; host and provider operators; another person holding the phone; an injected script; whoever later receives the packet.

**What stops each leak.** An identifier coming back from the reader: no key for it, `boundRead`, `scrub`, and the hospital strings are a lookup key only. Kindred storing or logging something: a local `MemoryStore`, the count as the only write, fixed-status errors, and a test that feeds garbage with `console.error` spied. The file's name, hidden details and leftover data: only a redrawn picture is uploaded, from I1. Text under a PDF cover box: PDFs are refused on the phone before I2 and turned into pictures after. A real bill reaching the server in demo mode: nothing a user drops is uploaded. A bill that carries instructions for the model: inline data only, schema-bound output, `validateDraft` on every letter figure. A guess printed as fact: the §6 rule and its tests. A failure dressed up as a blurry photo: the `failed` status. Demo data attached to a real bill: the live reader never uses the synthetic partner. Script exfiltration, or someone else on the phone: the site-wide policy, tab storage only, a 30-minute idle purge, erase.

**Residual risks, stated plainly.**
1. **An uncovered picture reaches Kindred's server and the reading service,** identifiers included, until layer three, and partly after it. Kindred holds it for one request; the reading service holds it under its own terms, which we disclose and cannot verify or shorten. Marker ink that shows through is read, and automatic covering misses handwriting and anything OCR cannot read; both fail quietly.
2. **Quasi-identifiers remain** in every result and letter: a hospital, a service date, codes and amounts can point to one visit. The mode is not anonymous and says so.
3. **The household assumption can be wrong.** One person is assumed in every run. The table shows the effect, the heading carries no percent when households differ, and no letter states a tier; a user who reads only the estimate can still take away the wrong figure.
4. **Coverage type and same visit are inferred, not asked.** A notice for another visit at the same hospital with matching codes and dates would pair as "likely". The letter asks the hospital to confirm when the pairing is weaker, and never names the coverage.
5. **A number can come back in the wrong field,** and nobody reviews it: a ZIP code passes as a five-digit service code, and a two-decimal amount is checked against nothing. The clippings show what was read; the letters ask rather than assert.
6. **The hospital match can be wrong or missing.** The atlas holds legal names for a fraction of US hospitals; a trade name on a letterhead often will not match, and then there is no help program and nothing the user can do but drop another page. A match is shown with its city and state and an "if this is not your hospital" line; the match rate is measured before go-live (task 18) and may prove too low. Accepted because the only fix is asking the user, which the founder ruled out.
7. **Two bills in one check are refused, not sorted,** and an insurer's statement gets plainer wording than a Medicare notice. Accepted: sorting pages into visits without asking is a guess.
8. **The name "Incognito"** sets an expectation that nobody sees or keeps anything, which is what the Chrome Incognito litigation turned on; S1 states the limit beside the button, and no trademark check has been done (**unverified**).
9. **The legal position is not settled by design.** Whether a tool that stores nothing is a vendor of personal health records under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, and how state health-data laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act apply, need counsel; FTC Act Section 5 applies to every sentence in §1. This is why real bills are gated.
10. **Smaller ones.** `'unsafe-inline'` remains in `script-src` because Next emits inline scripts. The atlas can be stale on whether a policy covers insured patients; every policy statement shows its quote and checked-on date. Whether a phone's file picker offers the camera without the `capture` button is **unverified**, which is why Snap's `Take photo` control is kept. `runPrivate` is a public endpoint that will call a paid model; the rate limit and spend cap are part of the gate. The device keeps the result until erase or the idle purge, and browsers can restore closed tabs; the printed packet, a saved PDF and the photos live wherever the user put them (S13 says so).

## 14. Decisions Made and Open Decisions
**Made**
1. **The founder's direction replaces the questions with labelled assumptions.** A guess never enters a letter (the letter leaves a blank, prints tick boxes for a pen or asks, and tests prove it). The household guess is the cautious one and sits beside the table for one to four. Coverage type is taken from the document the user dropped, not invented. The one value that cannot be guessed, the amount owed, stops the flow, and the only remedy is another file. Every figure of money is labelled Estimated.
2. One request, one action, on the server; the existing pipeline runs against a store that exists for that request only. Pages are held on the phone so files can arrive one at a time. The reader has no key for an identifier or for free text, except the hospital's name and city, which are used only to look the hospital up and are never returned.
3. Household size is never read, even where an income letter prints it; it is always assumed and always blank in the letter. Income is printed in the assistance application only as the figure a dropped page shows, with its period; the yearly household total is a blank.
4. With the demo reader nothing a user drops is uploaded; with any other reader only a redrawn picture is. That makes S3 and S8 true from the first day; PDFs wait for I2. Demo mode needs no key: three synthetic sets, and a table of reads the private demo reader alone knows.
5. The button reads "Incognito — Private Mode"; the naming risk is residual risk 8.

**Open, for the founder** (two)
1. **Who reads real bills.** Recommend Vertex AI: it has no free tier, so it cannot silently fall back to terms that allow training, and it is the route to shorter retention (how its retention works is **unverified** here). The paid Gemini Developer API is quicker to set up, but nothing in code can tell which terms a key is under, so it would need a named owner and a monthly billing check.
2. **The go-live gate for real bills.** Recommend shipping I1 and I2 in demo mode now and switching on only after task 17 is done, including this amendment to CLAUDE.md rule 1: "Private mode may receive a picture of a dropped page for one request, reads amounts, dates, service codes and the hospital's name from it, returns them to the device, and writes none of it to the store; the only stored value is a monthly count." Switching on without the gate puts unreviewed privacy claims in front of real patients.

## 16. What Changed on 2026-11-16
- **The founder's direction.** No typing and no changes by the user: they only drop files, as in Snap, and the mode copes with redacted data (the name and the like), on the assumption that income is shown on a dropped page.
- **Removed from the 2026-11-15 plan:** the type the numbers path and its on-device computation; the four questions; the explainer screen with tiles; the manual keep box and cover tool with its preview and gate screens; the hospital picker; the name and account fields on the letters screen; the regular-demo income read-only change; the exit to regular mode; the Kindred support-phone flag. Regular Snap is untouched except for the one button.
- **How the earlier red-team risks are contained without typing.** A guess that decides the outcome: it is shown with the chip Assumed, the household table shows the other answers, and no letter states it. A wrong fact in a signed letter: blanks, printed tick boxes and questions to the hospital, with tests on every dollar figure and on the words "Medicare" and "waiting". Hidden text and metadata in a file: only a redrawn picture leaves the phone, from the first phase. A real bill in demo mode: it never leaves the phone. A user who cannot get every page into one pick: pages are held and each new file re-runs the check. A wrong hospital: the lookup needs name and state and exactly one match, shown with its city. An outage reported as a blurry photo: a separate failure sentence. Unchecked provider terms: the terms record ships empty and blocks the live reader.
- **Changed after the second review of this plan:** promise sentences S1 to S16 reworded to say what we receive, not only what we use; S2, S3 and the reader terms moved out of the collapsed disclosure; Snap's `Take photo` control kept; rows T16 to T21, the help-program headings, the `failed` status and the stub reader added; the assistance letter quotes every policy sentence so it cannot vary with the household guess.
