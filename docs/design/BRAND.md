# Kindred Snap — brand sheet

A one-page reference in the shape of a brand-guidelines card. The full system is `DESIGN.md`.

## Colours

Main: Ink `#0C1C16` (text, dark grounds) · Paper `#FAF7EF` (grounds, text on ink) · Mist `#E9F8EF` (bars, panels) · Rule `#CBD6C9` (hairlines).

Accent: Pharmacy green `#007948` (primary action, confirmed) · Deep green `#005F3A` (pressed) · Mint `#A9E8C6` (done, highlighter) · Muted `#54655D` (captions).

Night and green grounds (`#040807`, `#006B40`) are reserved for the landing's theme card and the poster.

## Typography

Headings and body: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (400/500/700/800, Arial fallback). Figures, codes and dates: Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono (400/500, Menlo fallback). Base 18px, headings 700, one number on the poster at 800.

## Motif

The clipping: a strip of the real document with a mint highlighter stroke and its source caption. It is the logo's behaviour, not a logo mark: the wordmark "Kindred Snap" is set in the body face at 800 with tight tracking, no symbol.

## Voice

Plain, exact, unhurried. The words a daughter or son uses. Money is always estimated, confirmed or actual. Real and simulated are never confusable.
